### About the Experiment

Viral RNA/DNA extraction is the process of isolating the viral genome from clinical, laboratory, or field samples, such as serum, plasma, cell culture supernatant, or tissue homogenates. Viral nucleic acids are generally encapsulated within protein capsids, so efficient lysis is crucial for isolating and purifying them. The most common steps in the extraction process of viral RNA/DNA are chemical lysis of viral particles, inactivation of nucleases, and purification using silica-based spin columns.
