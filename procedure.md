### Procedure

**Preparation of lysate:**
* Add 200 μL of sample to the microcentrifuge tube and add 25 μL Proteinase K.
* Add 200 μL Lysis Buffer and mix by vortexing.
* Incubate at 56 °C for 15 minutes.
* Add 250 μL 100% ethanol to the tube and mix by vortexing.
* Incubate the lysate for 5 minutes at room temperature.   
* Add the above lysate to the Viral Spin Column in a collection tube.
* Centrifuge the column at 6800 × g for 1 minute. Discard the collection tube. Place the spin column in a new Wash Tube.
* Wash the column with 500 μL Wash Buffer (WII) with ethanol. Centrifuge at 6800 × g for 1 minute. Discard the flow through.
* Repeat the wash step.
* Centrifuge the spin column at maximum speed for 1 minute to remove any residual Wash Buffer.
* Elute with 10-50 μL nuclease-free water.
* Incubate at room temperature for 1 minute. Centrifuge the spin column at maximum speed for 1 minute to elute nucleic acids.
* To quantify the Viral RNA/DNA, blank the NanoDrop instrument using nuclease-free water. Load a small volume of the extracted viral RNA/DNA sample and measure absorbance.
